The plan for this report

Where this goes

Now

The monthly close, delivered like this

Every month: the one-page summary plus this interactive report in your portal — categorized, reviewed by a person, and reconciled to the bank statement’s printed ending balance before it can publish.

Next

The paperwork does itself

  • Bank and card statements read automatically — no more hand-keying — with every transaction categorized and queued for a human yes/no
  • Tie-out checks run on rules, not eyeballs: totals, duplicates, and odd amounts flagged the way an auditor would
  • Faster closes, fewer errors, and the accountant’s time spent on judgment instead of typing
Later

How you compare — real benchmarks, never guesses

A panel on this report showing your numbers against your industry and size:

  • Payroll, materials, equipment and margin as a share of revenue — against banker-grade industry data (the same RMA studies your bank uses when it reads your loan application) and IRS & Census industry figures
  • Shown as honest ranges and medians with the source named on every number — never a single made-up target
  • Your accountant interprets what the comparison means for your business
